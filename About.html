Our story

From one stall to a full depot

Twelve years of the same market, the same street, and a promise that hasn't changed.

2013

A single tile stall

Revolve Depot opened as a small tile stall inside Naze Building Materials Market, selling floor and wall tiles to homeowners renovating on a budget.

2016

Bathrooms & doors added

Customers kept asking where to find fittings and doors to match their tiles, so we expanded into water closets, basins, taps and a range of wooden and steel doors.

2019

Authorised cement dealer

We became an authorised dealer for Dangote, Lafarge and BUA, letting us supply cement at manufacturer-backed prices — by the bag or by the truckload.

2022

Trade accounts for contractors

We introduced tiered bulk pricing and dedicated support for contractors and developers managing multiple sites at once.

Today

One depot, four categories, one promise

40+ products across tiles, bathrooms, doors and cement — with the same depot, the same faces, and the same promise: build more, for less.

Our mission

Why "Build More For Less" isn't just a slogan

Building a home in Nigeria is already expensive enough. Our mission is to strip out every cost we can control on our end — so the savings land in your pocket, not ours. That means buying directly from manufacturers, keeping our overheads lean at one depot, and passing the difference on in our prices.

Direct sourcing

We buy directly from Dangote, Lafarge, BUA and verified tile/fittings importers — cutting out middlemen markups.

Transparent pricing

The price you see is the price you pay — no hidden fees added once you reach checkout or the counter.

Bulk pricing that scales

The more you buy, the less you pay per unit — tiered discounts on cement and tiles reward bigger projects.

One price, everywhere

What you see online is what you pay in person at Naze Market — no markup for convenience, no discount hidden behind a haggle.
